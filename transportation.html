Transportation

Find your way to and around Taniti.

Getting Around

Getting Here

Visitors can arrive by air or cruise ship. Rental cars are available at the airport.

Public Bus

Public buses serve Taniti City every day from 5:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m. Private buses serve other parts of the island.

Other Transport

Taxis are available in Taniti City. Visitors can also use rental cars and bicycles.

Walking and Cycling

Walking is practical in some areas. Helmets are required for bicycles.
