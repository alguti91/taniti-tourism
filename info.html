Visitor Information

Practical details for a smooth Taniti visit.

Good to Know

Currency

Taniti uses the U.S. dollar. Many businesses also accept euros and Japanese yen; and many accept major credit cards while banks offer currency exchange.

Electricity and Language

Power outlets use 120 volts, as in the United States. Many younger Tanitians speak fluent English; English is less common in rural areas, especially among older residents.

Health and Safety

Taniti has one hospital and several clinics. Violent crime is very rare, but visitors should take precautions against pickpocketing and other petty theft.

Hours and Holidays

Alcohol cannot be served or sold between midnight and 9:00 a.m. The drinking age is 18. Many attractions and restaurants close on national holidays, so plan ahead.
